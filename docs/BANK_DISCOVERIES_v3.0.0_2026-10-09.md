## Axos ONE · linked savings + checking

Research date: 2026-10-09. Rate effective date: 2026-10-09. Calculation: unsupported.

### Two accounts, four different rates (verified)

The savings headline depends on checking deposits and checking ADB. The 0.51% checking APY and 1.00% fallback savings APY are distinct.

**Why it matters:** Cash retained in checking has opportunity cost. The intro 5.00% rate is limited to the first $49,999.99.

**Check manually:** Read the qualification footnotes; verify cycles, eligibility and balances in both accounts.

[Axos ONE Savings: product / rate disclosure — Published APY, minimums, fees, qualification and product footnotes; accessed 2026-10-09](https://www.axosbank.com/personal/bank/axos-one)

### Authentication can depend on a U.S. mobile number (verified)

The new-account guide says verification codes cannot go to international numbers or landlines.

**Why it matters:** Travel or loss of the trusted phone may complicate access.

**Check manually:** Confirm recovery methods and transfer limits before moving a large balance.

[New account guide — Transfer limits, ONE minimums, biometrics and one-time verification codes; accessed 2026-10-09](https://www.axosbank.com/personal/support/new-account-guide)

## Wealthfront Cash Account

Research date: 2026-10-09. Rate effective date: 2026-10-01. Calculation: flat.

### App transfers and external pulls have different limits (verified)

Wealthfront-initiated ACH uses a $250,000/day cap; account/routing-number withdrawals use a separate personalized limit. Wires have no published amount cap.

**Why it matters:** Do not apply the ACH push cap to an external pull.

**Check manually:** Verify available funds, recipient eligibility and the wire delivery estimate.

[Limits on transfers and spending — Standard transfers, account/routing numbers, cards, checks and wires; accessed 2026-10-09](https://support.wealthfront.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048501391-Limits-on-transfers-and-spending) · [Send or receive money with wires — Eligible owners, destinations and delivery; accessed 2026-10-09](https://support.wealthfront.com/hc/en-us/articles/15967641307924-Send-or-receive-money-with-wires)

### Sweep ceiling is not personal coverage (verified)

The advertised $8 million eligibility uses program-bank allocation.

**Why it matters:** Existing deposits at the same program banks can reduce coverage.

**Check manually:** Review the current allocation and your other deposits; do not treat the headline ceiling as guaranteed insurance.

[Wealthfront Cash Account: product / rate disclosure — Published APY, minimums, fees, qualification and product footnotes; accessed 2026-10-09](https://www.wealthfront.com/cash)

### A blog date can be misleading (verified)

Wealthfront notes it updates APY information in some older posts without changing the publication date.

**Why it matters:** A current number on an old article is not evidence of the rate on its publication date.

**Check manually:** Only use explicitly dated rate statements or archived copies; do not fill missing months.

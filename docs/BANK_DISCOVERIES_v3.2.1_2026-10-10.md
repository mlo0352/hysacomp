## Axos ONE · linked savings + checking

Research date: 2026-10-09. Rate effective date: 2026-10-09. Calculation: unsupported.

### Two accounts, four different rates (verified)

The savings headline depends on checking deposits and checking ADB. The 0.51% checking APY and 1.00% fallback savings APY are distinct.

**Why it matters:** Cash retained in checking has opportunity cost. The intro 5.00% rate is limited to the first $49,999.99.

**Check manually:** Read the qualification footnotes; verify cycles, eligibility and balances in both accounts.

[Axos Bank: Axos ONE Savings: product / rate disclosure — Published APY, minimums, fees, qualification and product footnotes; accessed 2026-10-09](https://www.axosbank.com/personal/bank/axos-one)

### Authentication can depend on a U.S. mobile number (verified)

The new-account guide says verification codes cannot go to international numbers or landlines.

**Why it matters:** Travel or loss of the trusted phone may complicate access.

**Check manually:** Confirm recovery methods and transfer limits before moving a large balance.

[Axos Bank: New account guide — Transfer limits, ONE minimums, biometrics and one-time verification codes; accessed 2026-10-09](https://www.axosbank.com/personal/support/new-account-guide)

### Savings yield and checking conveniences need separate evidence (verified)

Axos ONE assigns the debit card to Checking. Early pay depends on payer timing. Support FAQ excludes two holidays and makes ATM rebates product-dependent.

**Why it matters:** A bank-wide feature list hides conditions and does not solve the dual-account yield model.

**Check manually:** Verify Checking card/rebate terms separately from Savings qualification, and confirm support availability for planned transfer dates.
